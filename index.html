About Us

The Story Behind CAKE N CREAM

At CAKE N CREAM, every cake is handcrafted with care, creativity, and premium ingredients. From wedding cakes and birthday surprises to custom celebrations and event decorations, we help turn special moments into unforgettable memories. Based in Tarore, Samba, we proudly serve families with fresh cakes, pastries, cookies, and personalized celebration services.

The Legend: Our Signature Biscoff Cheesecake

A creamy, lotus-infused masterpiece that has redefined indulgence. We fold authentic Biscoff spread into our secret cream cheese blend, resting on a toasted biscuit crust.
